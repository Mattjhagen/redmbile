What proof can be used?

Submit any of the following proof document types to show your eligibility:

Government Programs Proof
Income Proof

To prove participation in your qualifying program, you must provide a document that, at a minimum, includes:

  • Your name, or the name of your benefit qualifying person (BQP);
  • The name of the Lifeline-qualifying program, such as SNAP;
  • The government or Tribal program administrator or the managed care organization (MCO) that issued the document; and
  • An issue date within the last 12 months or a future expiration date that aligns with the benefit period.

These documents listed below could possibly be used if they meet the criteria listed above:

SNAP, CalFresh, Golden State Advantage, or WIC Card or award letter
